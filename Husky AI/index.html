About HUSKY AI

HUSKY AI is a student-built assistant for Northeastern University. It summarizes official pages and points you to the right place to act. No payments here — just fast answers and helpful links.

How it works: We look up a compact knowledge base of NU FAQs → answer concisely → include source links. If something’s missing, we say so and point you to the right office.
